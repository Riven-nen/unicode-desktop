# Preliminary Survey Results

Source: the updated Google Forms CSV. Item 1 has 37 responses; items 2–8 have 36 responses.

## Item means

| # | Statement | N | Mean | Interpretation |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | Loses focus when switching between the browser and the code editor | 37 | 3.00 | Neutral |
| 2 | Downloading and uploading assignment files is time-consuming | 36 | 3.19 | Neutral |
| 3 | Code does not run because of local compiler/environment issues | 36 | 3.28 | Neutral |
| 4 | Hard to track submitted and pending assignments | 36 | 3.06 | Neutral |
| 5 | Current programming workflow is inefficient and frustrating | 36 | 3.50 | Agree |
| 6 | Prefers one desktop workspace for instructions, code, run, and submit | 36 | 3.67 | Agree |
| 7 | Built-in editor and terminal would make lab activities easier | 36 | 3.69 | Agree |
| 8 | Dashboard for completion, grades, and deadlines would help | 36 | 3.75 | Agree |

## Grouped results

| Group | N item-responses | Mean | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Current workflow (items 1–5) | 181 | 3.20 | Neutral |
| Integrated workspace (items 6–8) | 108 | 3.70 | Agree |
| Overall | 289 | 3.39 | Neutral |

## Response distribution

| Group | Disagree (1–2) | Neutral (3) | Agree (4–5) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Current workflow | 17.1% | 49.2% | 33.7% |
| Integrated workspace | 3.7% | 44.4% | 51.9% |
| Overall | 12.1% | 47.4% | 40.5% |

## Interpretation

The current-workflow items obtained a group mean of 3.20 (Neutral), while the integrated-workspace items obtained 3.70 (Agree). The combined mean was 3.39, which is Neutral but close to the Agree threshold.

The results therefore lean toward the proposed direction: respondents were not strongly frustrated with the current workflow, but they generally agreed that a single workspace with a built-in editor, terminal, and progress dashboard would be useful. The 3.7% disagreement on the integrated-workspace items is notably small.

These are preliminary user-needs results, not post-use TAM results. A separate evaluation of Perceived Usefulness and Perceived Ease of Use is still needed for the completed system.
